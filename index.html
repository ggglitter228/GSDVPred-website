01

About GSDVPred

GSDVPred is a disease-specific machine-learning classifier developed to estimate the pathogenicity of single nucleotide variants in genes associated with genetic skeletal disorders. It combines multiple functional, conservation, and ensemble prediction features and reports a calibrated probability for variant prioritisation.

02

Recommended Usage

Use the GSDVPred score as supporting computational evidence during variant interpretation. The result should be considered together with population frequency, phenotype, segregation, functional evidence, inheritance pattern, and other ACMG/AMP criteria.

Not a standalone clinical decision toolA high score does not by itself establish a pathogenic clinical classification.

03

Limitations

This static website looks up variants in a published ClinVar prediction table. It does not annotate new variants or run the trained model. Coverage is limited to records in that file, and performance may vary across genes and variant consequences. GSDVPred does not replace expert review or clinical interpretation.

04

Genetic Skeletal Disorders

Genetic skeletal disorders are a heterogeneous group of inherited conditions affecting the growth, development, differentiation, and maintenance of bone, cartilage, and joints. GSDVPred focuses on variants in the curated GSD-associated gene set used by this project.

05

Score Interpretation

GSDVPred values are calibrated probabilities. The display ranges are presentation thresholds for prioritisation and are not clinical classifications. Comparison tools have distinct score scales and should not be compared numerically across methods.
